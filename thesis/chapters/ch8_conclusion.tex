\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{ch:conclusion}

\section{Conclusion}

A cascaded voice agent on a unified-memory edge device goes quiet for
seconds after the user stops. This thesis measured what that silence is made
of, priced the two ways of spending the listening window against each other,
and built a rule that chooses between them.

The silence belongs mostly to the recognizer. Speculating the reply, the
field's usual remedy, attacks a small stage, costs \ThinkerOverlap{} whenever
a draft overlaps the endpoint, and on spoken multi-step problems is rarely
usable early enough to finish. Speculating the transcript attacks the largest
stage. Its own overlap penalty can be removed by freezing the orphaned
decode, and a feasibility gate keeps it off utterances too short to pay. With
both, the listener cuts the median paired time to first audio on long
utterances by \PthreeRed{} ($n=\PthreeN$ items) and abstains on short ones.
A planner that reasons about when the user will stop does little better in
simulation, \PlannerDP{} at the median. On this device, the window belongs to
the listener, and a simple rule is enough to give it.

\section{Future work}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{An adaptive prior.} The gate stops early on utterances longer
  than its prior. A prior conditioned on the speech heard so far might
  capture what the planner's simulation finds there.
\item \textbf{Other tasks.} The thinker's negative rests on questions coming
  last. Tasks with early questions and long answers could reverse it, and the
  allocator's rule would then admit drafts.
\item \textbf{Other devices.} A GPU recognizer, a larger memory, or a
  different huge-page policy would change the stage shares and the tax. The
  method transfers; the numbers have to be re-measured.
\item \textbf{The system huge-page policy.} Only per-process levers were tried
  against the occupancy tax. A system-level policy change is untested.
\item \textbf{Live speakers.} The measurements use speech played in at real
  time. More speakers and spontaneous speech would test how far the
  endpointing and the gate carry.
\end{itemize}

\section{Generative-AI use}
\label{sec:genai-thesis}

Claude Code, Anthropic's agentic coding tool, was used throughout this work
under the author's direction. It ran Claude Fable~5 (2026-09-15), Claude
Opus~5 (2026-09-15 to 2026-10-06) and Claude Opus~5.5 (from 2026-10-06). It:
\begin{itemize}
\item wrote most of the software and analysis scripts;
\item executed the measurement runs the author approved;
\item drafted the pre-registrations, the lab-notebook entries, and the text of
  this thesis and of the related paper;
\item reviewed its own work through separate reviewer instances.
\end{itemize}
The author designed the study, approved every pre-registered prediction, made
every decision, approved each run, and verified the text. Every measured or
simulated value in this thesis is produced by a script from the logs;
registered bars and set sizes are stated as designed.
